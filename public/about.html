About The Brief

📰 What is The Brief?

The Brief is a free, open live news aggregator that brings together breaking stories, live video, live radio and podcasts in one clean, fast interface.

No account required. No paywalls. No tracking. No ads (yet). Just news from the sources you trust, updated continuously.

🌍 Coverage

12 categories including Top Stories, World, Tech, Business, Science, Sports, Cars, Motorcycles, Stocks, Crypto, Vibe (Good News / Funny / Weird) and Media.

World news broken down by region: Europe, Asia, Americas and Middle East.

Sources include:

BBC New York Times The Guardian Al Jazeera Reuters TechCrunch Wired Ars Technica The Verge DW NHK CoinTelegraph ScienceDaily 20+ more

📡 Media Hub

Beyond articles, The Brief includes a full Media tab with live video news from BBC, Al Jazeera, DW, Reuters and AP — live radio from 13 stations across 5 countries — and the latest podcast episodes from NPR, BBC, TED and more.

A persistent mini-player lets you keep listening while you browse.

🔒 Privacy first

The Brief does not collect any personal data, require registration, or track your reading habits. All content is fetched from public RSS feeds and YouTube channels.

Your preferences (theme, bookmarks) are stored only in your own browser's localStorage — never on our servers.

We care about privacy beyond our own product. For private browsing we recommend NordVPN, and to keep your accounts secure, NordPass.

👨‍💻 Built by

The Brief was built by Pedro Esteves, a frontend developer and digital trust & safety professional based in Barcelona, Spain.

Built with React, Netlify serverless functions and a genuine frustration at having 15 browser tabs open just to read the news.
